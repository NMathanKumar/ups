Title:
Attendance & Working Hours Policy

Category:
HR

Document Type:
Policy

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
Standard working hours at Apex Enterprise Inc. are 40 hours per week, Monday through Friday. Core collaborative hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, during which employees are expected to be available for team meetings, communication, and real-time collaboration.

Important Rules:
1. Employees must notify their line manager and HR before 8:30 AM if they are unable to attend work due to illness or an emergency.
2. Unexcused tardiness or failure to report for 3 consecutive days without notification is treated as job abandonment.
3. Flexible starting hours between 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM are allowed with manager concurrence, provided 8 working hours are completed daily.

Employee Action:
Report unscheduled absences immediately via email to absence@apex-enterprise.com and message your direct manager on Slack.

- How many hours per week am I expected to work?
- How do I report an unexpected absence or sick call?
