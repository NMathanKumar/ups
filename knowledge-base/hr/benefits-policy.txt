Title:
Employee Benefits & Compensation Overview

Category:
HR

Document Type:
Policy

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
Apex Enterprise Inc. offers comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance effective on Day 1 of employment for all full-time staff. The company matches 100% of employee 401(k) retirement contributions up to 5% of base salary. Employees also receive a $50 per month wellness stipend for gym memberships, fitness equipment, or mental health applications.

Important Rules:
1. Open enrollment for benefits changes occurs annually in November.
2. Life event changes (e.g. marriage, birth) allow benefits updates within 30 days of the event.
3. Expense receipts for the $50 wellness stipend must be submitted in the HR Portal by the 25th of each month.

Employee Action:
Access the Benefits Portal at hr.apex-enterprise.com/benefits to view plan details, enroll dependents, or submit monthly wellness stipend reimbursement claims.
