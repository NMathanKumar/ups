Title:
Annual Leave & Paid Time Off Policy

Category:
HR

Document Type:
Policy

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
Apex Enterprise Inc. provides full-time employees with 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at a rate of 2.08 days per completed month of service. In addition to annual leave, employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per year for illness or medical appointments. Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following calendar year, but carried-over days must be used by March 31 of that year.

Important Rules:
1. Annual leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance.
2. Leave requests must be approved by the employee's direct manager via the HR Portal.
3. Unused sick leave days do not carry over into the new calendar year and cannot be cashed out.

Employee Action:
To submit a leave request, log into the HR Portal (hr.apex-enterprise.com), select "Time Off", select the leave type (Annual Leave or Sick Leave), specify dates, and click "Submit for Approval".

Questions this document can answer:
- How many annual leave days do I get?
- How many sick leave days are provided per year?
- Can I carry over unused leave days into next year?
- How far in advance must I request annual leave?
