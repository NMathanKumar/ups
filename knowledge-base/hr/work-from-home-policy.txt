Title:
Work From Home (Remote Work) Policy

Category:
HR

Document Type:
Policy

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
Apex Enterprise Inc. supports flexible work arrangements. Full-time employees are eligible to work remotely (work from home) for up to 3 working days per week, provided their job role is designated as eligible for hybrid work. Core working hours during remote work days are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in the employee's local time zone.

Important Rules:
1. Prior manager approval in writing or via the HR Portal is mandatory before working remotely.
2. Working remotely without prior manager approval is considered an unexcused absence.
3. Remote work requires a secure, high-speed internet connection and an active company VPN session.
4. Working remotely from an international location for more than 10 consecutive days requires formal HR and Legal clearance.

Employee Action:
To request remote work days, submit a "WFH Request" in the HR Portal at least 24 hours prior to the requested remote day, specifying the date(s) and confirming manager verbal or written approval.
