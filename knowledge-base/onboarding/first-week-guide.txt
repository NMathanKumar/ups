Your first week at Apex Enterprise Inc. is structured to introduce you to company culture, team workflows, and enterprise tools.

Weekly Schedule:
Monday (Day 1): 9:00 AM Welcome Breakfast | 10:30 AM Hardware & IT Setup | 2:00 PM HR Orientation
Tuesday (Day 2): 10:00 AM Team Standup Introduction | 11:30 AM Buddy Sync | 2:00 PM System Architecture Overview
Wednesday (Day 3): 10:00 AM Product Demo Session | 1:00 PM Security Training Module | 3:30 PM Manager 1-on-1
Thursday (Day 4): 10:00 AM Team Collaboration & Shadowing | 2:00 PM Tooling & Access Setup
Friday (Day 5): 10:00 AM Weekly Team Retrospective | 3:00 PM First-Week Reflections & Manager Check-in
