Content:
Welcome to Apex Enterprise Inc.! This checklist outlines key activities to complete during your first 30 days.

Day 1 Tasks:
- Complete HR tax and direct deposit forms in HR Portal.
- Set up SSO password and Duo Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Collect and inspect company laptop from IT Helpdesk.
- Attend HR Orientation Webinar at 2:00 PM.

First Week Tasks:
- Meet with line manager to set 30-day goals and review team roadmap.
- Complete mandatory Security Awareness Training (due within 30 days).
- Set up Slack profile, photo, and notification preferences.
- Shadow your assigned onboarding buddy on 2 team projects.

First Month Tasks:
- Complete 30-day check-in review with line manager.
- Submit initial professional development learning plan.

- What should I complete during my first week?
- What documents do I need during onboarding?
- What are the Day 1 onboarding requirements?
