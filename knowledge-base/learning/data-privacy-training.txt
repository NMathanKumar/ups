Title:
Data Privacy & Compliance Training

Category:
Learning

Document Type:
Training Requirement

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
Apex Enterprise Inc. requires Data Privacy & GDPR/CCPA Compliance Training for all personnel who handle customer data, employee records, or confidential intellectual property.

Important Rules:
1. New hires in HR, Product, Engineering, and Customer Operations must complete training within 45 days of start date.
2. Annual renewal training is required by November 15 each year.
3. Minimum passing score is 85%.

Employee Action:
Access learning.apex-enterprise.com and select "Data Privacy Fundamentals" under your assigned curriculum.
