Mandatory Cybersecurity Awareness Training 2026

Category:
Learning

Document Type:
Training Requirement

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
The Annual Cybersecurity Awareness Training 2026 is mandatory for all full-time, part-time, and contractor staff at Apex Enterprise Inc. The course covers phishing recognition, password hygiene, remote work security, and reporting security incidents.

Important Rules:
1. New hires must complete the Security Awareness Training within 30 days of their start date.
2. Existing employees must complete annual refresher training by September 30 each year.
3. Passing criteria: Score 80% or higher on the final assessment quiz (up to 3 retakes allowed).
4. Failure to complete mandatory security training by the deadline will result in temporary suspension of network and email access.

Employee Action:
Log into the Learning Portal at learning.apex-enterprise.com, click "My Mandatory Courses", and launch "Annual Cybersecurity Awareness 2026".
