VPN Connection & Troubleshooting Guide

Category:
IT Support

Document Type:
Guide

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
Apex Enterprise Inc. uses GlobalProtect VPN (ApexSecure Global VPN) to provide secure access to internal applications, code repositories, and network shares. All remote work and off-site connections require an active VPN tunnel.

Important Rules:
1. Connecting to VPN requires your SSO username, password, and Duo MFA push approval.
2. If VPN drops or fails to connect, follow these troubleshooting steps in order:
   Step 1: Fully quit and restart the GlobalProtect VPN client.
   Step 2: Verify your local home WiFi or hotspot connection is active.
   Step 3: Switch gateway location in GlobalProtect from "Auto-Detect" to "vpn-backup.apex-enterprise.com".
   Step 4: Clear the client cache by clicking Settings -> Clear Cache, then restart your laptop.
3. If VPN remains disconnected after troubleshooting, submit an IT Support ticket.

Employee Action:
To log an IT support ticket for VPN issues, visit the IT Service Portal (itsm.apex-enterprise.com) or email it-support@apex-enterprise.com. Support response time for VPN outages is within 2 hours.
