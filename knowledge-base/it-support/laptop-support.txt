Title:
Laptop Repair & Hardware Support Policy

Category:
IT Support

Document Type:
Policy

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
Apex Enterprise Inc. provides standard laptop hardware (MacBook Pro 14" or Dell XPS 15) to all full-time employees. Laptops are automatically scheduled for hardware refresh every 36 months (3 years).

Important Rules:
1. For hardware failures, battery swelling, or physical damage, report the issue to IT Support immediately.
2. Temporary loaner laptops are available at all corporate office IT Helpdesks within 4 hours of request.
3. Remote employees will be shipped a replacement loaner laptop via overnight express delivery upon submitting an approved hardware ticket.

Employee Action:
Log a hardware incident ticket at itsm.apex-enterprise.com under "Hardware -> Laptop Support" and indicate whether you require an in-person loaner or overnight shipping.

Questions this document can answer:
- What should I do if my laptop breaks or has a hardware issue?
- How often are company laptops replaced or refreshed?
- How do remote employees get a replacement loaner laptop?
- How fast can I get a loaner device at the office?
