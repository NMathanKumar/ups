Title:
Self-Service Password Reset Guide

Category:
IT Support

Document Type:
Guide

Last Updated:
2026-01-15

Content:
Company account passwords for Apex Enterprise Inc. must be reset every 90 days. Passwords must be at least 14 characters in length and include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character (e.g. !@#$).

Important Rules:
1. Never share your password or MFA codes with anyone, including IT support personnel.
2. Accounts auto-lock after 5 consecutive incorrect password attempts.
3. Locked accounts automatically unlock after 15 minutes, or can be unlocked immediately via the Self-Service Password Reset Portal.

Employee Action:
To reset your password or unlock your account:
1. Navigate to identity.apex-enterprise.com/reset.
2. Enter your employee email address.
3. Approve the Duo MFA verification prompt sent to your registered mobile phone.
4. Enter your new password and confirm.
